# Prepare a new industrial study

Define the investment boundary, product grade and unit, market geography, target buyers, physical site, decision date and evaluation horizon. Where relevant compare options such as smaller scale, phased capacity, alternative site or process, and the without-project case. Use comparable scope and service levels. Assign each material gap to a source or next check.

## Market and sales

- Describe intended uses, grades, buyer acceptance and substitutes. Estimate demand from dated buyer, trade, industry or official data. Cross-check top-down estimates with achievable bottom-up sales when possible. Note gaps in product-code mapping, geography and coverage.
- Distinguish buyer interest, qualification, contracted sales and projections. Estimate reachable customers, volume by segment, channels, concentration, seasonality and the competitor supply pipeline.
- Build delivered and ex-works price comparisons on the same grade, date, freight, tax and payment terms. Use realized net price in the model. Link the ramp to customer qualification, capacity and sales evidence.

## Technical, site and implementation

- Describe process alternatives, major equipment, line bottlenecks, availability, rate, yield, shift patterns and ramp. Check that stable and first-year throughput follow those constraints. Reconcile raw feeds to saleable output, rejects and by-products where data permit.
- Check land/lease, layout, inbound and outbound logistics, raw material availability and quality, electricity, water, fuel, labor and maintenance. Distinguish planned connections and allocations from confirmed usable capacity.
- Identify installation and commissioning milestones, lead times, performance acceptance, operating dependencies and material contingencies. Link construction, ramp and delay to spending, initial working capital and earliest sale/collection.
- Identify material permits, safety and environmental issues, licensing conditions and related capex, operating costs, or timing. Consult project-specific official sources and qualified evidence when needed. Specify current status and supporting documents.

## Assemble the report

Lead with the decision, material findings and evidence limitations. Follow with project/options, market, technical and execution basis, investment and financing, operating economics and cash flows, scenarios and risks, and decision conditions. Include assumptions and source locators. If the analysis requests broader economic effects, support them and distinguish them from investment returns.

If one or more critical commercial, technical or financial inputs remains unsupported, deliver pre-feasibility with open items, proposed validation and only supported metrics. Where the data allow, link the final figures to the editable model.
